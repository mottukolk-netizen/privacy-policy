Privacy Policy

Hindi To English Translator
Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Hindi To English Translator respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, why it may be processed, how third-party services may be involved, and what choices are available to you.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Hindi To English Translator.

By using the application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with the practices described in this Privacy Policy, you may stop using the application.

2. Information You Provide

Hindi To English Translator does not require users to create an account in order to use the application's basic translation functionality.

When you enter text into the application, the text may be processed for the purpose of providing the translation requested by you.

We do not intentionally request information such as your full name, home address, phone number, government identification number, or payment information through the basic translation interface.

3. Translation Text

The primary purpose of this application is to translate text between Hindi and English, including supported Hindi and Hinglish-style input.

When you request a translation, the text entered by you may be sent through the translation service or API used by the application. This processing is necessary to generate and return the requested translation.

Translation text is used for providing the translation functionality and is not intentionally collected by us for the purpose of creating advertising profiles.

Users should avoid entering highly sensitive, confidential, financial, authentication, or other private information into the translator.

4. Translation Service and Third-Party Processing

The application may rely on third-party technology or online translation services to process translation requests.

When translation text is transmitted to such a service, the service may process the information according to its own privacy policy, terms, security practices, and applicable laws.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party service providers. Users should review the applicable privacy documentation of those providers where appropriate.

5. Translation History

The application may provide a History feature that allows users to access previous translations.

Translation history may be stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of providing this feature.

Locally stored history can be deleted using the deletion controls provided by the application.

If the application is uninstalled, locally stored application data may also be removed according to the behavior and settings of the Android operating system.

6. Text-to-Speech

The application may provide a Listen feature that reads translated or entered text aloud.

This feature may use the text-to-speech engine available on your Android device. Processing performed by the device's text-to-speech service may be subject to the policies of the provider of that service.

7. Advertising

Hindi To English Translator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the application.

Advertising services may process certain information associated with the device or advertising activity. Depending on applicable settings, region, consent, and advertising configuration, this may include information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interaction information, and information used for advertising measurement, fraud prevention, or ad delivery.

Google and its advertising partners may process information according to their applicable policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, users should review Google's applicable privacy documentation.

8. Advertising Choices and Consent

Advertising and privacy choices may vary depending on the user's location, applicable laws, device settings, and the configuration of the advertising services used by the application.

Where legally required, applicable consent mechanisms may be provided before certain types of advertising-related processing take place.

9. Device and Technical Information

Certain technical information may be processed automatically when the application or third-party SDKs operate on your device.

Depending on the services and SDKs integrated into the application, this may include information such as device type, operating system version, application version, IP address, advertising identifiers, crash or diagnostic information, and general app interaction data.

Such information may be used for purposes including providing services, maintaining security, preventing fraud or abuse, troubleshooting, measuring advertising performance, and improving service reliability.

10. Permissions

Hindi To English Translator is designed to request only permissions that are necessary for features implemented in the application.

If a future version of the application requires additional permissions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe the corresponding data practices.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the application.

Where supported by the relevant service, information transmitted between the application and online services is sent using secure HTTPS connections.

However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore avoid submitting highly confidential information through the application.

12. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

Translation history stored locally on your device remains available until you delete it through the application, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application, subject to Android system behavior.

Data processed by third-party services is subject to the retention practices and policies of those services.

13. Data Deletion

Users can delete locally stored translation history through the application's available history controls.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information that may have been processed by us, you may contact us using the email address provided below.
