Privacy Policy

Hindi To English Translator
Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Hindi To English Translator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our Android application, including translation, text-to-speech, translation history, and advertising features.

1. Information We Process

The app may process the text that you enter when you request a translation. This text is used to provide the translation service requested by you.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or home address in order to use the basic translation features.

2. Translation Service

When you request a translation, the text you enter may be transmitted to the translation service or API used by the application so that the requested translation can be generated.

Translation data is used for providing the requested translation functionality and is not intentionally used by us for personalized advertising.

3. Translation History

The application may store translation history locally on your device so that you can access previous translations.

You can delete saved translation history using the history controls provided within the application.

4. Text-to-Speech

The application provides a Listen feature that can read Hindi or English text aloud. Text may be processed by the text-to-speech engine available on your Android device.

5. Advertising

Hindi To English Translator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and use certain information, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information related to advertising and app usage, in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

We do not intentionally ask users to click advertisements. Users should never click an advertisement solely for the purpose of supporting the application.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services to provide translation, advertising, analytics, or other technical functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. Where supported by the services we use, data transmitted between the application and online services is sent using secure HTTPS connections.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Translation history stored locally by the application remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application, subject to the behavior of your Android device and application settings.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion practices of those services.

9. Children's Privacy

Hindi To English Translator is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for using online services. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the application.

10. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use translation, text-to-speech, and other optional features provided by the application.

You can also delete locally stored translation history through the application where the feature is available.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the application's features, services, or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
